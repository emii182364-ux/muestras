    FAQ

    Other questions? Just ask us on LINE.

    We already have a site. Do we need a new one?

    Not necessarily. We start with a free check — often improving speed, mobile and SEO makes a clear difference. We only suggest a rebuild if it's needed.

    Can it connect to our current booking system?

    We'll assess it. If you already use a booking or queue system we'll try to connect it; if not, you can use our online booking.

    Will the content follow medical advertising rules?

    We write following the Medical Care Act's advertising rules, avoiding exaggeration, comparisons or guaranteed results. The clinic approves the final text before launch.

    Who owns the domain and site?

    Yours. The domain, content and files belong to the clinic and can be fully transferred if you change providers.

    How long until it's live?

    A typical clinic site takes 2–4 weeks, depending on pages and features. The proposal includes a clear timeline.
